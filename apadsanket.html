🛡️ Safety Guide

Simple, practical guidance for the hazards most relevant to Nagpur.

🌊 Flood

Before

  • Keep emergency supplies ready.
  • Know nearby higher ground.

During

  • Move to a safe, higher location.
  • Avoid flowing water and electrical hazards.

After

  • Follow official instructions.
  • Avoid contaminated water.

🔥 Fire

During fire

  • Move away from the fire.
  • Avoid smoke and stay low if needed.
  • Call emergency assistance.
  • Never use elevators.

Remember

Do not re-enter a burning building for belongings.

⚡ Lightning

During lightning

  • Move indoors.
  • Avoid isolated trees.
  • Stay away from open areas and water.

Also useful

Guides can be extended for heatwave, accidents, industrial and crowd emergencies.
